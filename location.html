KAFD

King Abdullah Financial District — Riyadh’s glass-and-steel business quarter, with towers, a monorail and landscaped wadi walks at its heart.

Kingdom Centre

The city’s best-known silhouette: a 302-metre tower crowned by a sky bridge, with a luxury mall and hotel at its base on King Fahd Road.

Al Faisaliah Tower

Riyadh’s first skyscraper, recognisable by its golden glass sphere, home to offices, a hotel and fine dining above the Olaya skyline.

Boulevard City

An open-air entertainment district of promenades, restaurants, theatres and seasonal events — the centre of Riyadh’s nightlife.

Al Masmak Fortress

A clay-and-mudbrick fortress in old Riyadh, now a museum telling the story of the city’s founding.

King Fahd Stadium

The national stadium, crowned by its iconic tensile-fabric roof, host to football’s biggest matches and major concerts.
